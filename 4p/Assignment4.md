## Assignment: Data Project - Statistical Modeling and Interpretation: A Group Assignment

This assignment focuses on developing and presenting a statistical model based on prior exploratory data analysis (EDA). Students will work in groups to achieve the following:

-   Model Selection and model buiding: Choose an appropriate statistical model, considering the nature of the data and research question. Justify the selection.

    -   

    -   Presentation: Prepare a concise presentation summarizing the EDA, model, results, and conclusions. The presentation should be well-organized and accessible.

-   Model Interpretation: Analyze and interpret the model’s output. Identify key findings and their implications.

-   The presentation should last approximately 15 minutes, followed by a 5-minute Q&A session. Each group is expected to submit a written report detailing their methodology (with code) and findings.
